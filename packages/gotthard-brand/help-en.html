Help

Gotthard is a secretarial assistant for medical practices, clinics and hospitals. It works directly on the files in your workspace folder: it reads reports, drafts letters, fills in forms and organises documents. It does not make diagnoses.

Getting started

Put your documents in the workspace folder (menu Gotthard → Open workspace folder), then describe the task in the chat and name the files or folders involved.

Examples

  • Sort the scans in the Eingang folder into the patient folders and rename them by date and document type.
  • Write a letter to Hans Weber confirming his next appointment, using our letter template.
  • Summarise Claire Dubois' discharge letter in five points.
  • Fill in the form in the Forms folder with the details from the patient dossier.
  • Build an Excel list of all cost approvals with their validity dates.

Approvals

The assistant only works inside the workspace folder. It asks before running a program, fetching a web page or saving a note to the practice memory. Your original files are never overwritten.

Practice memory

The practice profile and the notes you approved are included in every conversation. You can edit them in .gotthard/memory.md.
